# Product boundaries (always apply)

- This is a coordination tool, not a clinical tool. Never add diagnosis, triage, treatment advice, consent capture for procedures, or any medical data field.
- Never add: AI or automatic sign translation, speech-to-text, in-app chat or message read states, payments or billing, interpreter commissions, session recording, camera or microphone capture beyond the device check page, face recognition.
- Not for emergencies. Patient pages always show the not-for-emergencies notice.
- Staff make the final assignment. Accepted is not booked. A help request is not an appointment and its wording never says booked.
- Remote or video interpreting requires patient consent and a policy that allows it for the visit's risk category. High-risk visits without a qualified interpreter show a blocking banner; the clinician decides, never the product.
- A relative present is never treated as the interpreter for clinical communication. A patient may decline an interpreter; record the informed choice and respect it.
- If a request conflicts with these boundaries, stop and ask a human.
