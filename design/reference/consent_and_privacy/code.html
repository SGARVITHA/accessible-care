Hospital Privacy Notice

Version 2.4 • Updated January 2025

Sample Hospital keeps your healthcare information private. We only share details needed to arrange sign language interpreters for your doctor visits.

1. Data Protection: Your medical records stay securely stored within Sample Hospital systems under clinical compliance laws.

2. Limited Sharing: Certified interpreters only receive appointment coordinates and communication preferences. No clinical test results or treatment history are shared.

3. Immediate Rights: You have the right to request a complete export of your preferences or delete your accessibility file at any registration desk.
